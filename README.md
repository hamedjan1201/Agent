## 🚀 What it does

Describe your vibe → get 3 perfect restaurant options instantly.

Example:
> romantic dinner under $100 in SF

Returns:
- Top 3 restaurants  
- Why each fits your situation  
- Price + address  
- ⭐ Rating  
- Direct website link  

---

## 💡 Why it’s different

Not a search tool — a **decision engine**.

Instead of browsing, you get clear, actionable choices.
